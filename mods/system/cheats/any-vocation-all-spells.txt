# name: Every vocation can use every spell and ability
# enabled: no
# category: Party
# about: Spells and abilities show up in the menus whatever the vocation (and the weapon), as long as the character knows them, and every character keeps the abilities of all 26 skill books (Swordcraft in Summary ... Secrets of the Shield, Warrior's Workbook ... Luminary's Lore) without carrying the book. Use "Learn every spell and ability" (Party page) once to teach everything. Both together: any class casts any spell and uses any ability, in the field and in battle.
# How: a character's vocation (record + 0x950) decides which spells the menus list: 0 means "every learned spell, every ability". The vocation is looked at in four places and each is told it is 0:
# the "is this spell on the vocation's list" check (main 0209ac28), the spell list of the field menu (ov002 02157294) and of ov026 (021dcae8), and the battle Abilities list (ov000 0217c404).
# The skill books (items 22265..22290, item type 0x1F, ability at item + 0x14): two loops go over them, ov026 021dc978 and ov004 0215ebdc. A book in the
# character's own bag (8 items at record + 0x454) teaches its ability (main 02083acc), a book not there takes it away again (main 02083b28). The branch
# to "take it away" (beq) becomes a nop in both, so every character learns every book's ability whether or not they carry the book. Switched off,
# the next pass of either loop takes the abilities of the books a character doesn't carry away again, as the game always does.
