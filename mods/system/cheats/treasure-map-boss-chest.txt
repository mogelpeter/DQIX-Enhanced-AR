# name: Treasure map bosses always drop their chest
# enabled: no
# status: untested
# replaces: Treasure Map Boss Always Drop Chest
# about: The boss at the bottom of a treasure map (grotto) always gives its special drop. Other monsters and the boss's ordinary drops are not touched.
# How: the game's drop routine (ov023 021f454c) looks the fight's map up in a table of special drops (a 10-byte record per map, found in fp).
# Bit 14 of the record makes the third drop slot a sure thing (chance 100), bit 15 the second slot (021f47c0 / 021f47dc). The two branches
# that skip those lines when the bit is clear (021f47bc, 021f47d4) are taken out, so in a map fight with a record both slots are sure.
# Fights without a record (every ordinary battle) are not changed. The earlier version took out the "no drop" branch after the roll
# (021f47f0), which made every drop of every monster in every battle sure.
check 021F454C E92D4FF8
patch 021F47BC 0A000001 E1A00000
patch 021F47D4 0A000001 E1A00000
